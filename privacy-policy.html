Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026. This policy explains how KakoBuy Spreadsheet handles privacy for a static informational website.

Information We Collect

This site is designed as a static resource. We do not require accounts, checkout, passwords, or database-backed profiles. If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, message, links, and correction details.

Local Form Behavior

The contact form is frontend-only. It can prepare a local status message in your browser, but it does not submit to a server by itself. Do not include sensitive personal information in guide suggestions or correction requests.

Cookies and Analytics

The current static files do not require cookies for core browsing. If privacy-friendly analytics are added later, they should be used only to understand aggregate page performance and guide interest, not to sell personal data.

External Links

Some pages link to third-party websites. External sites have their own privacy practices, content policies, and tracking behavior. Review those policies before submitting information or relying on external tools.

Data Retention

Messages sent by email may be retained as long as needed to respond, resolve correction requests, maintain editorial records, or comply with applicable obligations.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@kakobuyspreadsheet.com.
